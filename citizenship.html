How the civics test works

Study: 128 questions (2025 version, USCIS form M-1778).

At the interview: the officer asks 20 questions. You need 12 correct to pass.

Age 65+ with 20+ years as a permanent resident: the officer asks 10 questions from the shorter starred list (marked ★ 65/20 below). You need 6 correct.

Cost: this page and the app practice are free. No sign-up, no download.

When a question lists several answers, you only need to give one of them. If you are also learning English in Somali, the academy’s free lessons take you from the basics to interview English, step by step.

All 128 civics questions and answers — with Somali explanations

These are the official questions and answers published by USCIS (form M-1778, 2025 version). The Somali text under each question is the academy’s own explanation to help you learn.

Principles of American Government

1. What is the form of government of the United States?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Republic
  • Constitution-based federal republic
  • Representative democracy

Maraykanku waa jamhuuriyad: dadka ayaa doorta madaxda u hadasha, boqorna ma jiro. Saddexda jawaabood mid ka mid ah ayaa kugu filan.

2. What is the supreme law of the land? ★ 65/20

Answer: (U.S.) Constitution

Constitution-ku (dastuurka) waa sharciga ugu sarreeya dalka. Sharci kasta oo kale waa inuu isaga waafaqo.

3. Name one thing the U.S. Constitution does.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Forms the government
  • Defines powers of government
  • Defines the parts of government
  • Protects the rights of the people

Constitution-ku wuxuu dhisaa dowladda, wuxuu qeexaa awoodaheeda iyo qaybaheeda, wuxuuna ilaaliyaa xuquuqda dadka. Hal mid sheeg.

4. The U.S. Constitution starts with the words “We the People.” What does “We the People” mean?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Self-government
  • Popular sovereignty
  • Consent of the governed
  • People should govern themselves
  • (Example of) social contract

“We the People” waa saddexda eray ee Constitution-ku ku bilaabmo. Macnahoodu waa: awoodda dalka dadka ayaa iska leh, iyaga ayaana is xukuma.

5. How are changes made to the U.S. Constitution?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Amendments
  • The amendment process

Constitution-ka waxaa lagu beddelaa “amendment” — wax-ka-beddel ama kordhin si rasmi ah loogu daro.

6. What does the Bill of Rights protect?

Answers (you only need one):

  • (The basic) rights of Americans
  • (The basic) rights of people living in the United States

Bill of Rights waa tobanka amendment ee ugu horreeya. Waxay ilaaliyaan xuquuqda aasaasiga ah ee qof kasta oo Maraykanka ku nool.

7. How many amendments does the U.S. Constitution have? ★ 65/20

Answer: Twenty-seven (27)

Ilaa hadda Constitution-ka 27 jeer ayaa wax laga beddelay. Tirada xusuuso: twenty-seven.

8. Why is the Declaration of Independence important?

Answers (you only need one):

  • It says America is free from British control.
  • It says all people are created equal.
  • It identifies inherent rights.
  • It identifies individual freedoms.

Declaration of Independence waa qoraalkii lagu dhawaaqay in Maraykanku ka xoroobay Ingiriiska, in dadku siman yihiin, xuquuqna ku dhasheen.

9. What founding document said the American colonies were free from Britain?

Answer: Declaration of Independence

Declaration of Independence — ku-dhawaaqistii xornimada — ayaa sheegtay in gumeysigii Maraykanku ka xoroobay Britain.

10. Name two important ideas from the Declaration of Independence and the U.S. Constitution.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Equality
  • Liberty
  • Social contract
  • Natural rights
  • Limited government
  • Self-government

Labada qoraal waxay xambaarsan yihiin fikrado waaweyn sida sinnaan (equality) iyo xorriyad (liberty). Laba ka dooro liiska.

11. The words “Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness” are in what founding document?

Answer: Declaration of Independence

Erayadan caanka ah — nolol, xorriyad iyo raadinta farxadda — waxay ku qoran yihiin Declaration of Independence.

12. What is the economic system of the United States? ★ 65/20

Answers (you only need one):

  • Capitalism
  • Free market economy

Dhaqaalaha Maraykanku waa capitalism: ganacsiga waxaa iska leh dadka iyo shirkadaha, suuquna waa xor.

13. What is the rule of law?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Everyone must follow the law.
  • Leaders must obey the law.
  • Government must obey the law.
  • No one is above the law.

Rule of law waxay ka dhigan tahay: qofna sharciga kama sarreeyo. Madax, dowlad iyo shacabba waa inay sharciga raacaan.

14. Many documents influenced the U.S. Constitution. Name one.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Declaration of Independence
  • Articles of Confederation
  • Federalist Papers
  • Anti-Federalist Papers
  • Virginia Declaration of Rights
  • Fundamental Orders of Connecticut
  • Mayflower Compact
  • Iroquois Great Law of Peace

Qoraallo hore ayaa saamayn ku yeeshay Constitution-ka. Kan ugu fudud ee la xusuusto waa Declaration of Independence.

15. There are three branches of government. Why?

Answers (you only need one):

  • So one part does not become too powerful
  • Checks and balances
  • Separation of powers

Dowladda waxaa loo qaybiyay saddex laamood si aan midkoodna u noqon mid awood xad-dhaaf ah leh — mid walba kuwa kale ayuu ilaaliyaa (checks and balances).

System of Government

16. Name the three branches of government.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Legislative, executive, and judicial
  • Congress, president, and the courts

Saddexda laamood: legislative (Congress — sharci-dejinta), executive (madaxweynaha — fulinta) iyo judicial (maxkamadaha — garsoorka).

17. The President of the United States is in charge of which branch of government?

Answer: Executive branch

Madaxweynuhu wuxuu hoggaamiyaa laanta fulinta — executive branch.

18. What part of the federal government writes laws?

Answers (you only need one):

  • (U.S.) Congress
  • (U.S. or national) legislature
  • Legislative branch

Sharciyada federaalka waxaa qora Congress-ka, oo ah laanta sharci-dejinta.

19. What are the two parts of the U.S. Congress?

Answer: Senate and House (of Representatives)

Congress-ku wuxuu ka kooban yahay laba aqal: Senate iyo House of Representatives.

20. Name one power of the U.S. Congress. ★ 65/20

Answers (you only need one):

  • Writes laws
  • Declares war
  • Makes the federal budget

Congress-ku wuxuu qoraa sharciyada, wuxuu ku dhawaaqaa dagaal, wuxuuna sameeyaa miisaaniyadda federaalka. Hal mid sheeg.

21. How many U.S. senators are there?

Answer: One hundred (100)

Waxaa jira 100 senator — gobol kasta laba.

22. How long is a term for a U.S. senator?

Answer: Six (6) years

Senator-ka waxaa loo doortaa lix sano markiiba.

23. Who is one of your state’s U.S. senators now?

Answer: Answers will vary. [District of Columbia residents and residents of U.S. territories should answer that D.C. (or the territory where the applicant lives) has no U.S. senators.]

Jawaabtu waxay ku xiran tahay gobolka aad deggan tahay. Baro magaca mid ka mid ah labada senator ee gobolkaaga, kana hubi senate.gov ka hor wareysiga.

This answer can change — it names a current official.

24. How many voting members are in the House of Representatives?

Answer: Four hundred thirty-five (435)

House of Representatives wuxuu leeyahay 435 xubnood oo cod leh.

25. How long is a term for a member of the House of Representatives?

Answer: Two (2) years

Xubinta House-ka waxaa loo doortaa laba sano markiiba.

26. Why do U.S. representatives serve shorter terms than U.S. senators?

Answer: To more closely follow public opinion

Maadaama laba sano kasta la doorto, representatives-ku waxay si dhow ula socdaan waxa dadku rabaan.

27. How many senators does each state have?

Answer: Two (2)

Gobol kasta, weyn ama yar, wuxuu leeyahay laba senator.

28. Why does each state have two senators?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Equal representation (for small states)
  • The Great Compromise (Connecticut Compromise)

Laba senator gobolkiiba waxay gobollada yaryar siinaysaa cod la mid ah kan kuwa waaweyn. Heshiiskaas waxaa la yiraahdaa Great Compromise.

29. Name your U.S. representative.

Answer: Answers will vary. [Residents of territories with nonvoting Delegates or Resident Commissioners may provide the name of that Delegate or Commissioner. Also acceptable is any statement that the territory has no (voting) representatives in Congress.]

Jawaabtu waxay ku xiran tahay degmada (district) aad ku nooshahay. Magaca wakiilkaaga ka raadi house.gov adigoo gelinaya ZIP code-kaaga.

This answer can change — it names a current official.

30. What is the name of the Speaker of the House of Representatives now? ★ 65/20

Answer: Visit uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates for the name of the Speaker of the House of Representatives.

Speaker-ku waa guddoomiyaha House of Representatives. Magacu wuu is beddeli karaa — ka hubi uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates ka hor wareysigaaga.

This answer can change — it names a current official.

31. Who does a U.S. senator represent?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Citizens of their state
  • People of their state

Senator-ku wuxuu matalaa dhammaan dadka gobolkiisa.

32. Who elects U.S. senators?

Answer: Citizens from their state

Senator-ka waxaa doorta muwaadiniinta gobolkiisa.

33. Who does a member of the House of Representatives represent?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Citizens in their (congressional) district
  • Citizens in their district
  • People from their (congressional) district
  • People in their district

Xubinta House-ku waxay matashaa dadka degmadeeda (congressional district).

34. Who elects members of the House of Representatives?

Answer: Citizens from their (congressional) district

Waxaa doorta muwaadiniinta degmadaas ku nool.

35. Some states have more representatives than other states. Why?

Answers (you only need one):

  • (Because of) the state’s population
  • (Because) they have more people
  • (Because) some states have more people

Gobolka dadkiisu bato ayaa hela representatives badan; tirada waxaa lagu saleeyaa tirada dadka.

36. The President of the United States is elected for how many years? ★ 65/20

Answer: Four (4) years

Madaxweynaha waxaa loo doortaa afar sano.

37. The President of the United States can serve only two terms. Why?

Answers (you only need one):

  • (Because of) the 22nd Amendment
  • To keep the president from becoming too powerful

Amendment-ka 22aad ayaa xaddiday laba xilli, si aan madaxweyne u noqon mid awood xad-dhaaf ah yeesha.

38. What is the name of the President of the United States now? ★ 65/20

Sheeg magaca madaxweynaha hadda xilka haya. Doorasho ka dib wuu is beddeli karaa — ka hubi uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

This answer can change — it names a current official.

39. What is the name of the Vice President of the United States now? ★ 65/20

Sheeg magaca madaxweyne-ku-xigeenka hadda xilka haya. Wuu is beddeli karaa — ka hubi uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

40. If the president can no longer serve, who becomes president?

Answer: The Vice President (of the United States)

Haddii madaxweynuhu shaqada gudan kari waayo, madaxweyne-ku-xigeenka (Vice President) ayaa beddela.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Vetoes bills
  • Enforces laws
  • Commander in Chief (of the military)
  • Chief diplomat
  • Appoints federal judges

Madaxweynuhu wuxuu saxiixaa sharciyada, wuu diidi karaa (veto), wuxuuna yahay taliyaha guud ee ciidamada. Hal awood sheeg.

42. Who is Commander in Chief of the U.S. military?

Answer: The President (of the United States)

Commander in Chief waa taliyaha ugu sarreeya ciidamada — waa madaxweynaha.

43. Who signs bills to become laws?

Bill waa sharci-qabyo. Marka madaxweynuhu saxiixo ayuu sharci noqdaa.

44. Who vetoes bills? ★ 65/20

Veto waa diidmo: madaxweynuhu wuu diidi karaa sharci-qabyo uu Congress-ku u soo gudbiyay.

45. Who appoints federal judges?

Garsoorayaasha federaalka waxaa magacaaba madaxweynaha.

Laanta fulinta waxaa ka mid ah madaxweynaha, Cabinet-ka (golaha wasiirrada) iyo hay'adaha federaalka.

Cabinet-ku waa lataliyayaasha madaxweynaha — wasiirrada ayaa talo siiya.

Answers (you only need one):

  • It provides a compromise between the popular election of the president and congressional selection.

Electoral College waa habka lagu go'aamiyo cidda madaxweyne noqonaysa: gobol kasta wuxuu leeyahay codad doorasho.

51. What does the judicial branch do?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Reviews laws
  • Explains laws
  • Resolves disputes (disagreements) about the law
  • Decides if a law goes against the (U.S.) Constitution

Laanta garsoorku waxay dib u eegtaa oo sharxdaa sharciyada, waxayna xallisaa khilaafaadka sharciga.

52. What is the highest court in the United States? ★ 65/20

Supreme Court waa maxkamadda ugu sarreysa dalka.

Rights and Responsibilities

63. There are four amendments to the U.S. Constitution about who can vote. Describe one of them.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Citizens eighteen (18) and older (can vote).
  • You don’t have to pay (a poll tax) to vote.
  • Any citizen can vote. (Women and men can vote.)
  • A male citizen of any race (can vote).

Afar amendment ayaa ballaariyay xaqa codaynta: muwaadin kasta oo 18 jir ah ama ka weyn, rag iyo dumarba, wuu codayn karaa, lacagna lagama qaado. Hal mid sharax.

64. Who can vote in federal elections, run for federal office, and serve on a jury in the United States?

Answers (you only need one):

  • Citizens
  • Citizens of the United States
  • U.S. citizens

Saddexdan arrimood — codaynta doorashada federaalka, u-tartanka xil federaal iyo ka-qaybgalka jury — waxaa iska leh muwaadiniinta oo keliya.

Answers (you only need one):

  • Freedom of assembly
  • Freedom to petition the government
  • Freedom of religion
  • The right to bear arms

Qof kasta oo Maraykanka ku nool wuxuu leeyahay xorriyadda hadalka, diinta iyo isu-imaatinka. Saddex sheeg.

Answers (you only need one):

Answers (you only need one):

  • Derive citizenship (under conditions set by Congress)

Questions

How many questions are on the US citizenship test?

For the 2025 civics test you study 128 questions. At the interview the USCIS officer asks you 20 of them, and you must answer 12 correctly to pass. All 128 questions and answers are on this page, each with a Somali explanation.

What is the passing score for the civics test?

You need 12 correct answers out of the 20 questions the officer asks (60%). If you are 65 or older and have lived in the U.S. as a permanent resident for 20 years or more, the officer asks 10 questions from the shorter starred study list and you need 6 correct — those questions are marked ★ 65/20 above.

Is this citizenship test practice free?

Yes. This page and the full practice in the Somali English Academy app are completely free — no payment and no download. The app adds Somali audio, flashcards, reading and writing practice, and a full mock interview.

Is the citizenship test available in Somali?

The real test is in English, but you can study for it in Somali. Every question on this page has a Somali explanation, and the academy app gives you the citizenship test questions in Somali with audio, so you can learn what each question means before you memorise the English answer.

Which version of the test is this — 2025 or 2008?

This page is the 2025 version: the US citizenship test 128 questions from USCIS form M-1778. USCIS decides which version you take based on when you filed Form N-400. The academy app also includes the 2008 version (100 questions).
